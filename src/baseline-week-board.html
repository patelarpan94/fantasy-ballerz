Basics

This board shows the Indian Ballerz ESPN fantasy basketball league one matchup week at a time. All stats are real ESPN numbers; projections are ESPN's own and are always labeled ESPN projection.

  • Team picker (top left): choose any of the 10 teams. The board shows that team and its opponent for the week.
  • ⇄ Swap: flip to the opponent's view.
  • Week picker with ‹ › arrows: move between the 19 matchups. Today jumps back to the current week.
  • Updated shows when the data was last pulled from ESPN. The dot is green when it's fresh.

Matchup and days

  • Scoreboard: both teams' category totals for the week. The team ahead in a category is shaded green. W-L-T is the category score.
  • Played / Left: player games already played this week, and starter games still to come. Tonight's games move from Left to Played as soon as they go final.
  • Bench could start: extra games you'd get by moving bench players into open starting spots, checking each player's eligible positions.
  • Day cards: how many starters have a game each day for you and your opponent. Tap a day to filter everything to it; tap again to clear.
  • Counts use the lineup you saved in ESPN for each day, including future days. Bench, IR and OUT players aren't counted.
  • Set lineup ↗ (next to your team's name) opens ESPN's lineup page for the selected day, or today. Make your changes there, then come back and press Refresh. The opponent gets a View in ESPN link instead. A green can start ↗ tag also opens that day's lineup.

Tabs

Schedule
Each player's games this week by day. Played days show points/rebounds/assists; upcoming days show the opponent and tip-off time. Left is games left this week and Season is games left in the season. Tap a player for their game log.
Stats
This week totals, Season per game averages, or Projected: ESPN's full-season projection in bold next to the actual season so far in gray. Switch projections between Per game and Season totals.
Tracker
Charts of each category. This week shows running totals against your opponent. Over the season shows week by week or day by day for any range of weeks, compared with the league average or each week's opponent.
League
Every matchup this week, category ranks across the league, standings, and adds, drops and trades from the last 7 days.
NBA schedule
All 30 NBA teams by day for the week, with how many of your and your opponent's players are on each team, games left, and back-to-backs.
All players
Every rostered player plus the top 75 free agents. Filter by Season or Projected, Per game or Totals, the Free agents chip, rank (Top 25–150), position and team. Tap a column to sort. Tap + on up to 4 players to compare them side by side; the best number in each category is green.

The search box filters and highlights player names on Schedule, Stats, NBA schedule and All players.

Refresh

  • Refresh pulls live data from ESPN through the Claude desktop app's browser. It only works for the owner, on the desktop app.
  • After a refresh, the new data is saved and everyone who opens the board (including on a phone) sees it.
  • A refresh updates the whole week's box scores and lineups, standings, live NBA scores, and the injuries, season stats and projections of the two teams on screen.
  • The board only reads from ESPN. It never changes lineups or settings.

Terms

BE
Played while on the bench, so the stats didn't count.
DNP
Had a game but didn't play.
can start
Bench player with a game and an open starting spot he's eligible for that day.
vs / @
Home / away game.
B2B
Back-to-back: the second of games on consecutive days. Stars sometimes rest.
DTD / OUT
ESPN injury status: day-to-day or out.
W
Free agent currently on waivers.
% Ros
Percent of all ESPN leagues that roster the player.
Rank
ESPN's season rank on Season; ESPN's preseason rank on Projected.
Proj / Act
ESPN's full-season projection / actual stats so far.
