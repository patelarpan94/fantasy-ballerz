Basics

Indian Ballerz is a head-to-head categories league. Since version 2.0 (Oct 26, 2026) the app runs the league: set lineups, add, drop, claim off waivers and trade here, not on ESPN. Rosters started as an exact copy of our ESPN league on Oct 26. ESPN is only the source for NBA box scores, games, injuries and news. ESPN preseason projections are always labeled ESPN projection.

  • Team picker: look at any team. ⇄ next to it swaps to that team's opponent.
  • Week picker with ‹ ›: move between matchup weeks. Today jumps back to the current week.
  • Chat: the round button at the bottom right. League chat goes to everyone. Direct messages go to one team or the commissioner. A red count shows unread messages.
  • Your code: the app opens with a dial pad. Enter the 6-digit code the commissioner gave your team. Tap your team name at the top to log out.
  • Tap a player's name anywhere for his card: health, projection vs season vs last 7 days, and recent news. Tap elsewhere on his row for his game log. A blue dot by a name means news in the last 2 days.

Stats and Refresh

  • The dot and time at the top show when data was last pulled from ESPN. Green is current, amber is a few hours old, red is more than a day old.
  • Each pull brings in NBA box scores, tonight's scores, the injury report and news from ESPN. Rosters, lineups and settings live in the app and are never overwritten by a pull.
  • Refresh: for the commissioner it starts a pull in Claude's cloud. New data shows on everyone's devices a few minutes later. After pressing it, wait about 10 minutes before pressing again.
  • For everyone else, new data appears on its own whenever the commissioner refreshes. In the Claude desktop app, Refresh can also read ESPN directly.
  • Scores, standings and playoffs update from the pulled box scores automatically.

My Team

  • The header card shows your record, streak, league rank and chance to win this matchup, with shortcut chips to Schedule, Trades, Transactions, News and Analytics.
  • Your roster for the day you pick in the day strip (today if none), grouped into starters, bench and IR, with each slot as set that day.
  • Schedule shows each player's games this week: points/rebounds/assists for played days, the opponent and tip time for days ahead. Stats shows the full box score lines. Left is games left this week.
  • Tap a slot (MOVE, then HERE) to change your lineup. A player locks once his game starts. Auto-set today and Rest of matchup start your healthy players with games, best first.
  • can start and +N could start from bench mark bench players who have a game and an open slot they could fill. Tap Fix lineup to sort it.
  • Today is shown in green in the day strip and the schedule columns.

Matchup

  • The top card shows both teams with each side's win %: every category's odds come from its projected finish (box scores so far plus ESPN projections for games left), combined into the chance of winning more categories. Now is the live category score, projected the expected final.
  • The category board lists each category with the leader highlighted in green and the projected finish under each total.
  • Scoreboard: both teams' category totals for the week. Green is ahead. W-L-T is the category score.
  • Played / Left: starter games played and still to come. Bench could start: extra games available by moving bench players into open slots.
  • Rosters, Stats and Tracker show both teams side by side. Only stats from players in the starting lineup that day count.

Players

  • Free agents by default. Switch to everyone, rostered, or one team. Filter by rank and position, and sort by any column.
  • Tap + in the first column to compare up to 4 players side by side.
  • + adds a free agent (with a drop if your roster is full), − drops, ⇄ offers a trade. A dropped player goes on waivers for 24 hours; claims run Sundays at 8 AM ET in rolling order. Accepted trades go to a 48-hour league vote, and the commissioner can approve, veto or reverse any move.

League

Scoreboard
Every matchup this week and this week's category ranks.
Standings
Season record, season category totals and ranks, the playoff line and the bracket (top 4 make the playoffs, seeded by record).
Schedule
The 19 regular-season matchup weeks (copied from ESPN) and results.
News
The injury report for every rostered player (status, what it is, expected return, ESPN's note) and the latest ESPN NBA stories, filterable to rostered players or your team.
NBA schedule
All 30 NBA teams by day for the week, games left and back-to-backs.
Settings
Only the commissioner sees this tab. Settings are set in the app: 8 categories (FG%, FT%, 3PM, REB, AST, STL, BLK, PTS), PG, SG, SF, PF, C, G, F, 3 UTIL, 3 bench and 3 IR slots, 4 playoff teams, waiver rules and trade review. The commissioner also sets each team's access code here.

Analytics

  • Matchup forecast: this matchup's box scores so far plus ESPN per-game projections for each starter's remaining games, category by category.
  • Category heat map: every team's rank in each category, from projections or actual stats so far. Darker is better.
  • Strengths and punts: how far the selected team is above or below the league average in each category.
  • Games left, Heating up / Cooling off (last 7 days vs projection), Hot free agents and Streaming pickups (free agents with the most games left this matchup, weighted by value).

Draft

This season's draft happened on ESPN and its results were copied in. From next season the Draft tab is a live draft room with snake or straight order, a pick clock, queues and autodraft.

Badges

Icons next to a name mark last season's honors. Hover over one (or tap it on a phone) to see what it means.

Terms

BE
Played while on the bench, so the stats didn't count.
DNP
Had a game but didn't play.
can start
Bench player with a game and an open starting slot he's eligible for that day.
IR
Injured reserve. Up to 3 players; their stats don't count.
vs / @
Home / away game.
B2B
Back-to-back: the second of games on consecutive days.
DTD / OUT
ESPN injury status: day-to-day or out.
Rank
Season: our rank from this season's per-game category stats. Projected: ESPN's preseason rank.
Proj / Act
ESPN's full-season projection / actual stats so far.
