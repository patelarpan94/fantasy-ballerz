Start here

Indian Ballerz is a head-to-head categories league, and this app runs it. Make every move here, not on ESPN. ESPN only supplies NBA box scores, games, injuries and news.

  1. Log in: enter your team's 6-digit code on the dial pad. The commissioner gives it to you. To log out, tap your badge at the top.
  2. Set your lineup: on My Team, tap Auto-set today, or move players yourself (see Lineup).
  3. Pick up a player: on Players, tap + next to a free agent.
  4. Make a trade: tap ⇄ next to any player on another team.
  5. Talk: the chat button opens league chat and direct messages. A red number means unread messages.

Tap any player's name for his card: health, projection, recent stats and news. A blue dot by a name means news in the last 2 days.

Install & refresh

Put the app on your iPhone (about a minute):

  1. Open the league link the commissioner sent you in Safari. Chrome on iPhone can't add it as an app.
  2. Tap the ☰ menu button on the left of the address bar, then Share. On older iPhones the Share button is right in the bottom toolbar.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen (scroll down the list if you don't see it). Keep Open as Web App on and tap Add.
  4. Open the app from the basketball icon on your home screen from now on. It fills the screen like any other app.

On Android, open the link in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen.

Refresh: on any page, scroll to the top and pull down until it says Release to refresh, then let go. The app checks for new data right away, and for the commissioner it also starts a full ESPN pull.

  • You don't have to refresh. While anyone in the league has the app open, it pulls ESPN every minute during NBA games and every hour otherwise (injuries and news), and saves it for everyone.
  • New versions load on their own the next time you open the app. Nothing to reinstall.
  • While the league mirrors ESPN, a blue banner says so. Set lineups and make moves on ESPN (the Open ESPN button takes you there). They show up here after the next ESPN pull.

Set my lineup

  1. On My Team, pick a day in the day strip (today is green).
  2. Tap a player's slot (MOVE), then the slot he should go to (HERE).
  3. A player locks once his game starts. Only starters' stats count that day.
  • Auto-set today or Rest of matchup starts your healthy players with games, best first.
  • can start marks a bench player with a game and an open slot. Tap Fix lineup to sort it.
  • IR: only players listed OUT can go on IR. Their stats don't count while there.
  • The Schedule view shows each player's games this week. Stats shows box score lines, and Season charts shows trends.

Add, drop and waivers

  1. On Players, filter to free agents, a position or a rank, and sort by any column.
  2. Tap + to add. If your roster is full, the app asks who to drop. Tap − to drop.
  3. A player someone just dropped is on waivers. Tap + to put in a claim instead.
  • Claims run at set times, right on time even if nobody has the app open. When two teams claim the same player, the waiver order decides (see Rules).
  • Once a player's claims have run and nobody won him, he's a free agent anyone can add.
  • There's a limit on pickups each matchup. The add screen shows how many you have left.
  • A player added after his game starts joins tomorrow.
  • Tap + in the first column of Players to compare up to 4 players.

Trades

  1. Tap ⇄ next to a player, then pick what you'd give and get. Trade check shows how each side's categories change.
  2. The other team can accept, reject or send a counter offer. Offers show in chat and in League › Trades.
  3. An accepted trade goes to review. How long it lasts and how many veto votes stop it are set by the commissioner (see Rules). Without enough vetoes, it goes through when the review ends.
  4. After the trade deadline (see Rules), no new trades can be offered or accepted. Open offers expire.
  5. Players move the day after a trade goes through. The commissioner can approve, veto or reverse any trade.

Analytics › Trade finder suggests fair trades that help both teams.

Check my matchup

  • The top card shows the category score now and projected, and each team's chance to win. Odds come from box scores so far plus ESPN projections for the games left.
  • Each category shows both totals with the leader highlighted. Day buttons show how many starters each side has playing.
  • Advanced matchup (the switch under the score) shows day columns for each side.
  • Players shows both rosters, and Tracker charts each category through the week.
  • The team picker and week picker at the top show any team in any week. ⇄ jumps to that team's opponent.

Your app

  • Refresh: for the commissioner it starts a pull in Claude's cloud. New data shows on everyone's devices a few minutes later. Everyone else gets new data on its own.
  • The dot and time at the top show when ESPN data last arrived: green is current, amber is a few hours old, red is over a day old.
  • ◐ Display: the look (Classic or NEW ARP, with player photos and motion), light or dark theme, your NBA team's colors, and the layout (Auto, Phone style, Desktop or Two-pane). It's saved to your login.
  • Release notes: the folder button next to ? lists what changed in each version.

Terms

BE
Played while on the bench, so the stats didn't count.
DNP
Had a game but didn't play.
can start
Bench player with a game and an open starting slot he fits that day.
IR
Injured reserve, for players listed OUT. Their stats don't count.
vs / @
Home / away game.
B2B
Back-to-back: the second of games on consecutive days.
DTD / OUT
ESPN injury status: day-to-day or out.
Rank
Season: our rank from this season's per-game stats. Projected: ESPN's preseason rank.
Proj / Act
ESPN's full-season projection / actual stats so far. ESPN projections are always labeled ESPN projection.
⚠
That team's roster isn't legal (see Rules). Adds, claims, trades and lineup changes stay locked until it's fixed.
